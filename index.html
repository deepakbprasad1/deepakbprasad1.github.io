Research

Master thesis · FAPS Institute, FAU · 07/2026 – 01/2027

CAD-VLA: A CAD-Grounded Vision-Language-Action Model for Demonstration-Free Pick-and-Place of Unseen Industrial Parts

PyTorch · PaliGemma-3B / BridgeVLA · LoRA · NVIDIA Isaac Sim & Isaac Lab · cuRobo · GraspGenX · Franka Panda

68 %success on 300 held-out episodes with unseen parts
≈ 88 %of the perfect-goal ceiling (78 %), up from 16 % for the first model
−19 / −26points without the CAD / with a wrong CAD, so the model really uses it
0human demonstrations: 1,500 CAD parts, 62k grasp tests, 25k auto-labelled scenes
  • A keypose VLA takes the part's CAD file, fused RGB-D scene views and a language instruction, and outputs a 6D pick-and-place goal (grasp, resting face, yaw, position).
  • The CAD is the action space: the model scores stable faces and yaw angles generated from the CAD, plus physics-tested grasps. cuRobo plans the motion; a closed loop verifies and retries.
  • Fine-tuned PaliGemma-3B with LoRA plus new depth, 3D and CAD encoders, a heatmap decoder and a candidate scorer.
  • Tasks cover table, tray, part-on-part and peg-in-hole placement. Success stays stable when cameras move by up to 30 cm.

Thesis in progress, submission January 2027. Code and full results will be linked here once published.

Robot learning experiments · 2025 – 2026

Benchmarking VLA and imitation policies on real and simulated arms

NVIDIA GR00T N1.5 / N1.7 · ACT · SmolVLA · LeRobot-style low-cost arm · Isaac Sim

Before CAD-VLA I trained and compared existing policies on a low-cost 6-DoF arm and in simulation. These runs showed how much human demonstration data current VLAs need, which motivated a demonstration-free approach.

Project thesis (grade 1.3) & student research assistant · FAPS Institute, FAU · 2024 – 2025

Simulation of 3D Multi-Camera Calibration for Cameras with Non-Overlapping Fields of View

ROS · Gazebo · Blender (Python) · MoveIt · OpenCV · Isaac Sim

  • Built a ROS-integrated Gazebo simulation with configurable cameras, scripted robot motion and hard conditions (lighting, blur, defects) to benchmark calibration algorithms.
  • Automated the modelling of true-to-scale icosahedral calibration targets in Blender with Python, and generated synthetic datasets that improved calibration accuracy and robustness.
  • Simulated and real calibration agreed closely in intrinsic parameters and reprojection error, which validated the pipeline.
  • Also built a structured-light (camera + projector) simulation and a MoveIt calibration cell, and migrated the lab stack from ROS Noetic to ROS 2 Jazzy.
